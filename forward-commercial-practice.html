The practice at 50,000 feet
Most commercial interventions arrive too late.

As AI compresses reaction time across all industries, the window between an inflection signal becoming visible and that signal being priced in by competitors is collapsing to months, not years. By the time an organisation recognises that its model is obsolete or its position has eroded, the window for low-cost correction has closed.

We work with a small number of organisations at a time, retained as a practice. We begin before urgency, mapping commercial blind spots, building infrastructure — dynamic deal scoring, AI pricing models, revenue transparency frameworks — or restructuring how value is captured as business models shift. We detect inflection signals at the edges of the competitive arena before corner cases become crises.

The result is a transformed commercial posture, installed before the market forces the question. This is what we mean by forward.

Strategic architecture
Three intellectual threads, shaken together.

Each thread does a different job. Together they form something forward-facing.

Heffernan Uncharted
Provides the philosophical licence. The future is not foreseeable, so the practitioner's job is not prediction — it is rehearsal capacity. Clients don't need a forecast. They need their organisation to practise navigating ambiguity before it becomes a burning platform. The fractional model enables this: the practitioner holds simultaneous vantage points across multiple organisations, generating cross-pattern recognition that an embedded exec structurally cannot access.
McGrath Seeing Around Corners
Provides the operational mechanism: inflection point detection at the arena edge. Most organisations watch their own industry. The inflection is forming at the edges of adjacent arenas, where they are not looking. The practitioner's job is to sit at those edges on the client's behalf.
Toufani T-Labs · Lygometry · AQ
Provides both the measurability layer and the diagnostic entry point. Lygometry — from the Latin for measuring darkness — is the quantification of what we know we don't know. Applied commercially, it surfaces the blind spots that carry asymmetric downside if left unexamined. The Adaptability Quotient (AQ) then measures organisational capacity to respond, giving a baseline before the engagement and a measurable delta after. The result is accountability to a number, not an opinion.
