The Practice at 50,000 Feet
Most commercial interventions arrive too late.

As AI compresses reaction time across all industries, the window between an inflection signal becoming visible and that inflection being priced in by competitors is collapsing to months, not years. By the time an organisation recognises that its model is obsolete or its position has eroded, the window for low-cost correction has already closed.

We work with a small number of organisations at a time, retained as a practice. We begin before urgency, mapping commercial blind spots, building infrastructure such as dynamic deal scoring, AI pricing models and company AI revenue transparency frameworks, or restructuring how value is captured as business models shift. We detect inflection signals at the edges of the competitive arena and sometimes lead deal pursuits for deals that matter most, before corner cases become crises.

The result is a transformed commercial posture, installed before the market forces the question.

This is what we mean by forward.

Our Strategic Architecture
Four intellectual threads, shaken together.

Each thread does a different job. Together they form something forward-facing.

Heffernan Uncharted
Provides the philosophical licence. The future is not foreseeable, so the practitioner's job is not prediction. It is rehearsal capacity. Our customers don't need a forecast. They need their organisation to practise navigating ambiguity before the ambiguity becomes a burning platform. The fractional model enables this because the practitioner holds simultaneous vantage points across multiple organisations, and cross-pattern recognition that an embedded exec structurally cannot access.
McGrath Seeing Around Corners
Provides the operational mechanism: inflection point detection at the arena edge. The key question is what factors prevent organisations from anticipating and responding to disruptions. McGrath's answer is that they're watching their industry, not the edges of adjacent arenas where the inflection is forming. The fractional practitioner's job is to sit at those edges on our customers' behalf.
Toufani T-Labs / Exonomics™
Provides the measurability layer. The Adaptability Quotient (AQ) is defined as the ability to realise optimal outcomes in response to change. Critically, Toufani's Exonomics framework offers predictive models for the impact of accelerating technologies on individuals, businesses, and nations. That's the missing ingredient most commercial advisory lacks: a quantified baseline for organisational adaptability before the intervention, and a measurable delta after.
Agnotology The study of structured ignorance
Provides the epistemic architecture. The formal study of structured ignorance, what organisations don't know they don't know, is the diagnostic entry point. The practitioner's first act is not to audit the commercial strategy. It is to map the ignorance topology: where are the blind spots baked into the current model, and which of them carry asymmetric downside if they remain unexamined past a certain inflection?
